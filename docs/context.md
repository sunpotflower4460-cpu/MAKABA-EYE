# Historical and Geometric Context

## Purpose of This Document

This document distinguishes four separate layers that MAKABA-EYE intentionally keeps apart.

---

## Layer 1: Historical Merkavah / Merkabah

**Merkavah** (Hebrew: מֶרְכָּבָה, "chariot") is a concept from early Jewish mysticism.
It refers to the divine throne-chariot described in the vision of the prophet Ezekiel
(Ezekiel 1; 10). In Rabbinic and mystical tradition, the Merkabah became the central
object of mystical contemplation.

**Hekhalot / Merkabah literature** is a body of early Jewish mystical texts (roughly
3rd–7th century CE) describing ascent through heavenly palaces (hekhalot) to behold
the divine chariot. Key texts include *Hekhalot Rabbati*, *Hekhalot Zutarti*, and
*Sefer Hekhalot* (3 Enoch).

> This is a distinct religious and historical concept. MAKABA-EYE does **not** claim
> to visualise the historical Merkavah.

---

## Layer 2: Geometric "Merkaba" (Stella Octangula)

In modern new-age and sacred geometry contexts, the term "merkaba" is sometimes
applied to the **stella octangula** — two interlocking regular tetrahedra. This usage
is a modern symbolic association, not a historical one.

Mathematically, the stella octangula is:
- The **compound of two regular tetrahedra**
- The **only stellation of the regular octahedron**
- Its **convex hull is a cube**
- Its **intersection is a regular octahedron**

MAKABA-EYE implements and visualises this geometric object.

---

## Layer 3: Symbolic Reading

Symbols sometimes attached to the stella octangula include: centre, inversion,
boundary, transit, return, mediation, and protection. These are **interpretive
layers** added by contemporary practitioners of sacred geometry. They are not
historical facts and are not asserted as such by this application.
